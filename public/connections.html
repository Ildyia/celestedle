Rules of Connections

Sort the 16 Celeste elements into 4 groups of 4 that share something. Select 4 tiles, then press Submit. Each element belongs to exactly one group, and there is only one way to sort them all.

What makes a group

  • The same type (for example 4 characters)
  • A shared colour, or the same shape
  • Found in the same chapter
  • A fact only used in Connections, such as "gives liftboost": the group's name is revealed once you find it

Each group gets its own colour. The colour tells nothing about how hard the group is.

Mistakes

You can make 4 mistakes: the 4th one ends the game and shows the solution. "One away" means 3 of your 4 tiles belong together. In Hard, 4 tiles can share something without being a group: the other tiles can't all be sorted then. You are told so, and it still counts as a mistake. Submitting the same 4 tiles twice doesn't count as a mistake.

Normal and Hard

Normal: every group is clear-cut. No 4 tiles outside a group share a type or a fact, so nothing looks like a group without being one.

Hard: decoy groups are allowed. 4 tiles can share something and still not be a group. Mark tiles with colours to plan ahead: marks stay in your browser and don't submit anything.

Today's grid and practice

Each mode has one grid a day, the same for everyone. Today's ranking: fewest mistakes first, then fastest time. Practice grids are yours only: draw as many as you like, they don't count in the ranking.

Suggest a new tag

A tag is a fact some Celeste elements share, used to make groups. For example "gives liftboost" or "part of a major speedrun skip". The team will review your idea.
