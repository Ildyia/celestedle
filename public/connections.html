    Rules of Connections

    Sort 16 Celeste elements into 4 groups of 4 that share something. Each element belongs to exactly one group, and there is only one way to sort them all.

    1. 1Select 4 tiles you think go together.
    2. 2Press Submit. A right group is revealed with its name.
    3. 3Find all 4 groups before your 4th mistake.

    What makes a group

    Type4 characters, 4 hazards…
    Colour or shape4 blue things, 4 round things…
    Chapter4 elements found in Golden Ridge…
    TagA fact only used here, like "gives liftboost"

    Each group gets its own colour. The colour tells nothing about how hard the group is.

    When it's not a group

    • One away…3 of your 4 tiles belong together.
    • These 4 do share something…Real link, but with it the other tiles can't all be sorted. Mostly in Hard.
    • Not a group.Nothing links these 4.

    Each of these costs a mistake. Submitting the same 4 tiles twice doesn't.

    Hints

    • 💡 Kinds: what sort of link each group is (type, colour, chapter, tag…).
    • 💡 Category: the name of a group you haven't found yet, the tag first if there is one.
    • 💡 Pair: two tiles that go together.

    Each hint can be used once per grid: all 3 in Normal, only 1 in Hard. A hint doesn't make you lose, but it counts as a mistake in the rankings.

    Normal or Hard

    Normal

    Every group is clear-cut: nothing looks like a group without being one. At most one tag group per grid.

    Hard

    Decoy groups are allowed. Mark tiles with colours to plan ahead: marks stay in your browser.

    Today's grid and practice

    Each mode has one grid a day, the same for everyone, ranked by fewest mistakes, then fastest time. Practice grids are yours only: draw as many as you like, they don't count in the ranking.

    Suggest a new tag

    A tag is a fact some Celeste elements share, used to make groups. For example "gives liftboost" or "part of a major speedrun skip". The team will review your idea.
