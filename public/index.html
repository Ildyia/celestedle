Rules of Celestedle

Guess the secret element of the day by entering its name. You'll receive feedback based on its attributes.

Categories

  • Collectibles: Items collected in-game (strawberries, hearts...).
  • Characters: Characters of the story like Theo, Granny, Oshiro...
  • Environments: Entities without a major impact on the gameplay (Binoculars).
  • Mechanics: Entities that create the main gameplay (Crumble blocks).
  • Movement & Propulsion: Mechanics that you use to move around or gain speed (move block, zippers...).
  • Hazards: Obstacles that kill the player (Spikes, Spinners).

Chapters

The "CHAPTER" column indicates which level.s (amongst prologue, the A-sides, farewell) the element can be found in.

Colors Indicator

  • Correct (✓): Perfect match.
  • Partial (~): Guess is part of the answer.
  • Not Wrong (≠): Contains part of the answer + extra.
  • Wrong (✕): Incorrect attribute.

Hints

Click the "Hints" button to access 3 seeded hints based on the word of the day:


  • Opposite: Reveals the element with the most opposite infos to the word of the day
  • Secret Info: Reveals 1 correct attribute of the secret word (Type, Location, Colour, or Hitbox).
  • 1 Truth & 2 Lies: Reveals 3 attributes, one of them is from the secret word, the 2 other are a bait.

Each type of hint can only be used once per day.
