Celestedle 2.0 is here!

Hey everyone!

First, sorry for the long wait since the last update. This one took a lot more time than planned, because almost everything under the hood has been rebuilt. Here is what's new:

  • Hard mode: its own daily word, limited hints, tries and time, suggestions only after a few letters, and every guess must fit the clues you already found.
  • Profiles: pick a name, an avatar and a colour, and follow your own stats and streaks.
  • Leaderboards: daily, weekly and all-time, for both modes. You can hide your profile if you prefer.
  • Bug reports: similar reports show up while you type, so you can vote for them instead of posting the same thing.
  • Clearer rules for colours and shapes, words that can come back sooner, and a lot of bug fixes.

Your game is now saved on the server: use the recovery code in your profile to play on another device.

Rules of Celestedle

Guess the secret element of the day by entering its name. You'll receive feedback based on its attributes.

Type

  • Collectibles: items collected in-game (strawberries, hearts...).
  • Characters: characters of the story like Theo, Granny, Oshiro...
  • Environment: entities without a major impact on the gameplay (binoculars).
  • Mechanics: entities that create the main gameplay (crumble blocks).
  • Movement & Propulsion: mechanics that you use to move around or gain speed (move blocks, zippers...).
  • Hazards: obstacles that kill the player (spikes, spinners).

Chapter

Every level the element appears in, among the prologue, the A-sides and Farewell.

Colour

Only the main colours of the element are considered, not the background or the details. The selected colour(s) are the ones that are most visible and/or most important to identify the entity.
If the entity has multiple forms, the dominant color of each form is considered.
Example: Move blocks are entirely black in their default form, but they have an active form with strong green lines, and a breaking form with red lines. It also have some grey details. The selected colours of move blocks are therefore black, green and red.

Shape

  • Square: as wide as it is tall. Tiles element are square.
  • Rectangle: clearly longer in one direction. If an entity can be a square or a rectangle, it will be counted as rectangle (because square are a specific type of rectangle).
  • Round: Roundish form. Circles, ovals, circle with extra details etc.
  • Humanoid: a character with a body.
  • Irregular: any other silhouette, mostly non-rectangular polygons.

Colour indicators

  • Correct (✓): perfect match.
  • Partial (~): your guess is part of the answer.
  • Not wrong (≠): part of the answer, plus extra values.
  • Wrong (✕): nothing in common.

Hints

  • Opposite: the element with the most opposite attributes to the word of the day.
  • Secret Info: one value of the secret word, in a category you haven't found yet.
  • 1 Truth & 2 Lies: three statements, only one of them is true.

In normal mode, each hint can be used once per day. No hints in hard mode. A hint will always try to give you a useful clue (eg, "correct attribute" will not give you something you already know).

Hard mode

  • Its own secret word, different from the normal one.
  • No hints, limited tries and time.
  • Suggestions only appear after a few letters, and there is no full list (official synonyms work).
  • Every guess must fit the clues you already have: if your guess were the answer, all your previous guesses would have got the same colours. Guesses that don't fit are refused and don't count.
  • Once your Hard game has started, you can't switch back to Normal until it's over (win, loss or give up).

Rankings

Daily: fewest tries, then fastest time. Weekly and all-time: a win is worth 11 points minus the number of tries (1 minimum). Time is used as a tiebreaker.

A win on the very first try is pure luck: it is listed apart in the daily ranking (🍀), without a rank, but still scores its points for the weekly and all-time rankings. Your profile appears in the rankings once you've saved it at least once.
