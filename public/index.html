Celestedle 2.0 is here!

Hey everyone!

First, sorry for the long wait since the last update. This one took a lot more time than planned, because almost everything under the hood has been rebuilt. Here is what's new:

  • Hard mode: its own daily word, 1 hint, 8 tries, 5 minutes, no suggestions, and every guess must fit the clues you already found.
  • Profiles: pick a name, an avatar and a colour, and follow your own stats and streaks.
  • Leaderboards: daily, weekly and all-time, for both modes. You can hide your profile if you prefer.
  • Bug reports: similar reports show up while you type, so you can vote for them instead of posting the same thing.
  • Clearer rules for colours and shapes, words that can come back sooner, and a lot of bug fixes.

Your game is now saved on the server: use the recovery code in your profile to play on another device.

Rules of Celestedle

Guess the secret element of the day by entering its name. You'll receive feedback based on its attributes.

Type

  • Collectibles: items collected in-game (strawberries, hearts...).
  • Characters: characters of the story like Theo, Granny, Oshiro...
  • Environment: entities without a major impact on the gameplay (binoculars).
  • Mechanics: entities that create the main gameplay (crumble blocks).
  • Movement & Propulsion: mechanics that you use to move around or gain speed (move blocks, zippers...).
  • Hazards: obstacles that kill the player (spikes, spinners).

Chapter

Every level the element appears in, among the prologue, the A-sides and Farewell.

Colour

Only the dominant colour counts, the one that covers most of the element. Outlines and small details don't count. If the element is made of several distinct parts (a block and its track, a character and what it holds), each part adds its own dominant colour.

Shape

  • Square: as wide as it is tall.
  • Rectangle: clearly longer in one direction.
  • Round: a circle or an oval.
  • Humanoid: a character with a body.
  • Irregular: any other silhouette.

Colour indicators

  • Correct (✓): perfect match.
  • Partial (~): your guess is part of the answer.
  • Not wrong (≠): part of the answer, plus extra values.
  • Wrong (✕): nothing in common.

Hints

  • Opposite: the element with the most opposite attributes to the word of the day.
  • Secret Info: one value of the secret word, in a category you haven't found yet.
  • 1 Truth & 2 Lies: three statements, only one of them is true.

In normal mode, each hint can be used once per day.

Hard mode

  • Its own secret word, different from the normal one.
  • 1 hint, 8 tries and 5 minutes from your first guess.
  • No suggestions: type the full name (official synonyms work).
  • Every guess must fit the clues you already have: if your guess were the answer, all your previous guesses would have got the same colours. Guesses that don't fit are refused and don't count.

Rankings

Daily: fewest tries, then fastest time. Weekly and all-time: a win is worth 11 points minus the number of tries (1 minimum).
