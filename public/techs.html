Rules of Celestedle Techs

Guess the Celeste tech of the day by entering its name. Each guess shows how close it is to the answer, column by column. Short names work too: "super", "hyper", "wb", "bhop", "cb" or "theovator".

Columns

  • Category: Mechanics, Dash Tech, Dashless Tech, Entity Tech or Other Tech, like on the Celeste Wiki.
  • Difficulty: Passive, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced, Expert, GM, GM+ (grandmaster) or TAS (only really done in tool-assisted runs). A wrong difficulty shows ⬆️ when the answer is harder, ⬇️ when it is easier.
  • Utility: what it is mostly used for: Speed, Height, Distance, Stamina, Dash Recovery, Hitbox, Clipping, Leniency or Control.
  • Dash: whether you need a dash to do it.
  • Origin: Coded (the game has code for it), Derived (it comes from mixing other mechanics) or Glitch (a bug of the game).

Colours:

  • Right value
  • Wrong value (with an arrow for the difficulty)

Tech list based on the Celeste Wiki.

Hints

  • Opposite: the tech that shares the least with the answer.
  • Secret Info: one value of the answer you don't know yet.
  • 1 Truth & 2 Lies: three statements, only one is true.
  • Tech Info: pick any tech and see all its values.

Each hint can be used once per day, and hard mode allows fewer of them.

Normal and Hard

  • Normal: 3 hints, unlimited tries.
  • Hard: its own tech of the day. Every guess must fit the clues you already have, or it is refused and costs a try.

Rankings

Techs have their own daily rankings, and count in the weekly and all-time rankings with every other mode: see the rules of Celestedle for how a game is scored. Each hint counts as one extra try.
