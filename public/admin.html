Tags

A tag only goes into grids once it is complete: every element must have been sorted (ticked = it has the tag, unticked = it doesn't). An element added after the tag was last saved makes it incomplete until you sort it. It needs at least 4 ticked elements, and 40 at most. In A-Side, a grid never has exactly 4 tiles sharing something (a type, a colour, a tag…) unless they are one of its groups; 5 or more are allowed, the player sees one has to go.

Tag diagnostics

Draws test grids in the background (about 30 seconds, the site keeps running) to show which tags stop grids from being drawn. "Blocks" is the share of rejected draws a tag caused in A-Side: it would have made 4 tiles share it outside the groups (a decoy), or it had too few elements of its own once the other groups were placed. Tags that overlap a lot with another category, or are very big, block the most.
